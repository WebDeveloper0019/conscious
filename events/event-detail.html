About the Event

The Sustainable Living Summit 2025 is India's most comprehensive annual gathering of sustainability champions. Now in its 5th year, the summit brings together thought leaders, conscious brands, grassroots artisans, policymakers and engaged citizens for three days of inspiration, innovation and action.

This year's theme is "Circular by Design" — exploring how circular economy principles can transform Indian manufacturing, consumption and waste management at scale.
