About This Product

This exquisite Blue Pottery Vase is a treasure from the heart of Jaipur, crafted by master artisan Rajan Kumhar using techniques passed down through four generations. Each piece is hand-painted with the signature blue-and-white motifs that define Jaipur's legendary pottery tradition.

Made from a unique blend of quartz, powdered glass, and clay, Blue Pottery is distinct from regular ceramics — it's fired at lower temperatures and has a characteristically translucent quality. Every brushstroke is applied by hand, making each piece absolutely one-of-a-kind.
