Brand Story

Weaving a Conscious Future

EarthWeave Co. was born in 2018 from a simple belief: fashion can be beautiful and ethical at the same time. Founder Meera Joshi quit her corporate career to work with the handloom weavers of Barmer, Rajasthan — women whose centuries-old weaving skills were at risk of disappearing.

Today, EarthWeave partners with 340+ weavers across Rajasthan and Gujarat, using only natural plant-based dyes and 100% organic cotton. Every piece tells the story of the hands that made it.

"We don't just sell clothes. We sell stories — of women who weave their dreams into every thread." — Meera Joshi, Founder
