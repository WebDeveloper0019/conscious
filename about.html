What is Conscious India?

Preserving India's heritage and conscious culture. Empowering the people behind it.

Behind every conscious product is a person, a purpose and a story worth knowing.

We are building a connected ecosystem that helps conscious brands get discovered, artisans build stronger livelihoods, consumers make better choices, and Indian craftsmanship receive the recognition it deserves across India and the world.

Our Vision

A future where conscious consumption is the norm, not the niche, and Indian brands rooted in culture are celebrated on a global stage.

Our Mission

To bring India's conscious ecosystem under one roof, connecting sustainable brands, artisans and communities with the consumers who care.

The Beginning

It began with his mother. It grew into a larger purpose.

Deepesh Barua - Founder & COO

In 2024, Deepesh began his journey inspired by his mother and the challenges he witnessed women face while building independent livelihoods. His first purpose was simple: create better opportunities for women-led businesses.

Working with entrepreneurs, artisans and sustainable brands revealed a wider problem: India has incredible talent and craftsmanship, but many makers lack visibility, consistent growth and access to the right markets.

Is a pop-up enough? Is an online listing enough? Is one exhibition enough to build a sustainable business?
The answer was clear: not enough.

What was missing was a connected ecosystem - one that turns visibility into sustainable growth and takes Indian craftsmanship and skills to the world.

What Guides Us

The 11 Ethics of Conscious Indi

These principles guide the products we promote, the partners we welcome and the experiences we create.

01

Conscious Consumption First

We promote mindful purchasing by encouraging products that create positive environmental and social impact while reducing wasteful consumption.

02

Sustainability by Authenticity

Every product should contribute to a lower environmental footprint through responsible sourcing, production, packaging and logistics. We reject greenwashing.

03

Support for Indian Artisans & Heritage

We preserve India's traditional crafts, indigenous knowledge and artisan livelihoods through fair market access and equitable opportunities.

04

Fair Trade & Artisan Empowerment

We support fair opportunities, visibility and equitable earnings for artisans, craft communities, rural entrepreneurs and indigenous creators.

05

Vegan & Cruelty-Free Commitment

We promote clearly disclosed vegan and cruelty-free products that avoid animal-derived ingredients and animal testing wherever possible.

06

Circular Economy & Upcycling

We encourage upcycling, recycling, repairability and repurposing that extend resource life and reduce landfill waste.

07

Environmental Responsibility

Brands must strive to reduce emissions, water consumption and plastic use while protecting ecosystems, wildlife, soil and biodiversity.

08

Transparency & Traceability

Customers deserve to know where products come from, how they are made and who makes them. We promote ethical sourcing practices.

09

Inclusive Growth & Community Development

We foster opportunities for women-led enterprises, social enterprises, self-help groups, tribal communities and underrepresented creators.

10

Responsible Collaboration & Innovation

Technology and business innovation must create positive outcomes. Creators and partners must disclose partnerships and market products honestly.

11

Give Back to the Planet

Our initiatives should support environmental restoration, biodiversity conservation, artisan welfare and community development.

Our Story

Born to Change How India Shops

Conscious Indi began with one question: what if commerce could restore culture instead of erasing it?

The Beginning

A Potter in Jaipur Changed Everything

In 2021, our founders Arjun and Meera were travelling through Rajasthan when they met Rajan Kumhar — a fourth-generation Blue Pottery master who was struggling to sell his work at more than ₹200 per piece to middlemen, while the same pottery sold for ₹2,000+ in Delhi boutiques.

The math was brutal. The story was clear. And Conscious Indi was born — to close that gap, permanently.
